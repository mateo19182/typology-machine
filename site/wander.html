How this works

Matches use a visual model on object crops, computed in advance. They indicate resemblance, not the same physical object. Each step loads a real street photograph. Where matching landmarks are reliable, a small rotation aligns the photographs around the object. Otherwise, the object boxes provide the alignment. Photo edges may leave the frame; empty space is left empty.

Use Back to retrace your route. Next avoids photographs already visited where possible. The frame anchor, object size and matching mode travel with a shared link. Image sources: Apple Look Around and Google Street View.
