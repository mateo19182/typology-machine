How this works

Matches use a visual model on object crops, computed in advance. They indicate resemblance, not the same physical object. Each step loads a real street photograph and moves and scales it around the selected object. Photo edges may leave the frame; empty space is left empty.

Use Back to retrace your route. Next avoids photographs already visited where possible. The frame anchor and object size travel with a shared link. Image sources: Apple Look Around and Google Street View.
